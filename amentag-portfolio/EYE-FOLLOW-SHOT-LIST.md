# Shot list: the portrait that follows the mouse

The homepage portrait switches between 9 photos of you, depending on where the visitor's mouse is.
For it to look smooth, the 9 photos must match exactly except for where you look.

## Setup (same for all 9 shots)
- Camera on a tripod at eye level, about 1.5–2 m away. Don't move it between shots.
- Same seat, same clothes (the black hoodie), same lighting, same framing as your current portrait.
- Portrait orientation, 4:5. You'll export at 1200 × 1500 px.
- Stick 8 small notes on the wall around the camera: top-left, top, top-right, left, right, bottom-left, bottom, bottom-right. Keep them about 1 m from the lens.
- Keep your shoulders still. Turn your head slightly (about 10–15°) and move your eyes toward each note.
- Use a remote or the self-timer, or ask a friend to press the shutter.

## The 9 shots and their file names
The names describe where your eyes point **as seen in the photo**: "left" means the left side of the picture.

|---------------|------------------------------------------|

Tip: after shooting, check a few frames on screen. If a photo named "left" shows you looking toward the right of the picture, swap the left and right files.

## Editing
- Apply one identical edit (black and white, the same contrast) to all 9. In Lightroom, edit one and sync the settings to the rest.
- Crop all 9 the same way, so your face sits in the same place in every frame.
- Send them in any format (JPG is fine). I'll convert them to WebP and switch the site over.
