# Oussama Amentag portfolio (Blogger redesign)

Redesign of https://amentaag.blogspot.com/ with the same pages (Home, About me, My works, My resume, Contact me) and labels (Film, Photography, Projects).

- `t1/` is template T1: light/dark, viewfinder hero, film-strip cards.
- `t2/` is template T2: dark and cinematic, giant serif name with the portrait set into it, a projector-beam light that follows the mouse, page-cut transitions. It shares T1's images (`t2/img` links to `t1/img`).
- `t3/` is template T3: T2 plus project footage inside the name, a film-leader countdown intro, a scroll timecode bar, scrubbable project covers and magnetic buttons. Shares T1's images.
- `t4/` is template T4: T3 plus a showreel player (chapters per project, play/pause, timecode) and an interactive projection-mapping wall on a kasbah facade. Shares T1's images.
- `t5/` is template T5 (not chosen, kept for reference): the site is Oussama's video-editing suite. Home is an edit page (program monitor playing the showreel, media pool with label bins, inspector, draggable timeline with V2/V1/A1 tracks and client-quote markers that play as subtitles, J/K/L and space shortcuts). Resume is a career timeline with an inspector; Contact is a "Deliver" render-settings form. Shares T1's images.
- `t6/` is template T6 (not chosen, kept for reference): T4's content and look with full motion design built on GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger (loaded from cdnjs): a title-sequence intro once per visit (counter, name rising letter by letter, curtain wipe), headings rising word by word, photos wiping open with a slow zoom and parallax, a statement that lights up word by word on scroll, a pinned sideways-scrolling "Frames from the field" section, count-up numbers and a header that hides on scroll down. Everything is shown without motion when the visitor prefers reduced motion or the library fails to load.
- `t7/` is template T7: bright and sunlit (sand, ocean, sunset), wide Syne display type. A camera-iris intro opens on the site; the portrait sits in a round lens ringed by rotating role text; the background shifts from sand to ocean to sunset as you scroll; a giant word marquee reacts to scroll speed; project cards stack; testimonials drag; the resume line draws itself; a live Agadir clock sits in the hero and the contact band. Uses GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger from cdnjs and degrades to a static page without them.
- `t8/` is template T8: T7 plus a zoom-through-the-lens scroll (the hero pins, the lens grows to fill the screen, the name splits apart and the portrait dissolves into footage), a rack-focus on the portrait when the site opens, a 2.5x magnifying loupe over project photos, and menu words that decode on hover.
- `t9/` is template T9: a quiet white gallery style (thin Onest type, one coral accent, bracketed section labels, pill buttons) inspired by a Behance reference the owner shared, rebuilt with original layout and content. Hero: project stills in a slow 3D orbit around the name with an orbit line drawn through the page; works on a curved 3D wall with filters; a four-step commission process; about with the eye-follow portrait; testimonials; client grid; and the contact form floating inside a CSS 3D screening room hung with project stills.
- `t10/` is template T10, the current direction: T8 refined. Adds a curved wall of all 18 project frames (filters, drag, arrows), a four-step process whose line draws on scroll, a 3D screening-room contact form, hero letters that thin out near the cursor, and four colour palettes (Bone & clay default, Atlantic, Desert gold, Ink & cobalt) with an on-page switcher meant for choosing only; remove the switcher once a palette is picked. Also fixes the hero name not reappearing after scrolling back up (present in T8).
- Open any of them through a local web server, for example `python3 -m http.server` inside the folder.
